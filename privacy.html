WLAN Commander — Privacy Policy

WLAN Commander is a field diagnostic tool for Wi-Fi engineers. It collects a great deal of technical data about wireless networks, and essentially none of it reaches us. This page explains exactly what is gathered, where it is kept, and the few places it can go.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

The short version

We operate no servers for this app, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of any kind. Everything the app records stays on your device unless you deliberately send it somewhere — and the destinations it can reach are listed below. WLAN Geeks LLC does not receive your captures, your locations, or any record of your use of the app.

What the app collects

WLAN Commander is a network analyser, so most of what it records is about radio equipment rather than about you.

Location

Precise GPS position and altitude, a barometric floor estimate, and whether you were still, walking or driving. Used to tag observations, so a network heard from a moving car is not confused with one heard at a desk. Collected in the background when you are running a survey or capture.

Wi-Fi observations

Beacons, BSSIDs, SSIDs, signal levels, channel and capability information, and captured 802.11 management and control frames. This is the substance of the tool.

Other people’s devices

Wi-Fi analysis necessarily observes nearby equipment you do not own. Captures may contain the hardware addresses of third-party devices, the network names they search for, and authentication handshake material broadcast over the air. Handle captures accordingly.

Camera and room geometry

If you use the survey scanner, the camera and LiDAR build a floor plan. No photograph or video is saved or transmitted — only the resulting wall geometry.

Diagnostic imports

If you import an iOS sysdiagnose archive for analysis, it is parsed on your device. These archives contain operating-system and other applications’ logs; nothing is uploaded.

Credentials

SSH usernames and passwords for your own sensors, and any cloud API credentials you choose to enter, are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are used only to reach the equipment you point the app at.

Where data is stored

On your device. Captures, surveys, reports and their sidecar files live in the app’s own storage, which is exposed through the Files app so you can move your work where you want it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Where data can go

The app makes network connections to these places and no others:

Your own equipment

SSH and SFTP to the sensors, access points and capture devices you configure. This is the app’s main purpose. We are not party to it.

Speed and path tests

If you run the speed test or the multipath lab, the app contacts Cloudflare’s public speed endpoints, check.mptcp.dev and 1.1.1.1. These measure your connection; they carry no capture data.

Vendor cloud APIs

If you supply credentials for a vendor management cloud, the app talks to that vendor on your behalf to enrich access-point information. Optional, and only with credentials you provide.

Passpoint provisioning

If you open a provisioning link, the app fetches the profile from the address in that link and installs it using Apple’s standard Wi-Fi configuration prompt.

Emergency-location data

The Location Readiness feature examines how well a network would report a caller’s position to emergency services. That data is unusually sensitive, and the app is built so it cannot leave your device at all — the restriction is enforced mechanically at build time rather than by policy, and the app is incapable of placing or dialling a call. Reports are local files. Nothing is sent to any location server, and no map tiles are requested, because requesting a map would itself disclose the locations being examined.

What we do not do

No advertising. No tracking, and no data shared with data brokers or advertising networks. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No user accounts, and no sign-in. We do not build a profile of you, because we receive nothing with which to build one.

This website

If you use the contact form on our home page, the name, email address and message you type are sent to us so we can reply. That is the only personal information this site collects. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server access logs.

Children

WLAN Commander is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Your responsibilities

Wireless capture is regulated differently around the world. You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to analyse the networks you point this tool at, and for handling captures — which may contain other people’s device identifiers — lawfully.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
